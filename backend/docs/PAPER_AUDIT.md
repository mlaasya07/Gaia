## Critical project decision

The datasets have different units and labels:

- ZeMA: hydraulic machine cycles and component condition labels.
- Future Factories: assembly-line sensor/vision data and assembly anomalies.
- Google Cluster Data/SPECpower: computing workload and power profiles.
- Steel-powder/digital-twin papers: case-study data not included or available on request.
- World Models environments: game/racing observations and rewards.

Therefore Gaia should be a **multi-module system with separate dataset-specific models plus a shared simulator/state schema**, not one model trained on a falsely unified table. The bridge between modules should be explicit: each module emits normalized signals such as predicted machine-health risk, anomaly risk, expected energy, carbon intensity, backlog, and estimated latency. The coordinator consumes those signals in the simulator.
